Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Introduction

Bryte Tech Solutions LLC ("Bryte Tech," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit brytetech.solutions, contact us, or engage us for consulting or technology services — how we use that information, and what choices you have.

Bryte Tech provides IT consulting, event technology implementations, AI solutions, and related professional services to businesses and organizations. This policy covers both our website visitors and our consulting clients.

Information We Collect

From website visitors:

  • Contact form submissions — your name, email address, company name, and message content when you fill out the contact form on our website.
  • Email correspondence — messages you send to our inquiry or support email addresses.

From consulting clients and their organizations:

  • Project information — business requirements, system configurations, workflow documentation, and technical specifications shared during an engagement.
  • Platform credentials and access — login credentials, API keys, or system access provided for the purpose of delivering contracted services (e.g., Salesforce, RainFocus, Help Scout, Zendesk, DocuSign, Brightcove, Vimeo).
  • End-user data encountered during implementation — event registrant records, CRM contacts, or attendee data that may be accessed while configuring or troubleshooting your systems. This data is accessed only as necessary to complete the engagement and is never retained beyond project completion.
  • Business communications — emails, meeting notes, Slack messages, and other correspondence related to active engagements.

We do not use tracking pixels, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics platforms. Our website does not set any cookies.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and evaluate potential projects.
  • Deliver the IT consulting, integration, or AI services you have engaged us to perform.
  • Configure, test, and troubleshoot technology platforms on your behalf (e.g., Salesforce, RainFocus, AI support agents).
  • Send administrative communications related to an active engagement — invoices, project updates, scheduling, and deliverables.
  • Maintain records necessary to support ongoing or future engagements.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not use client data to train AI models or improve our own products without explicit written consent.

Client Data and Confidentiality

We treat all client data — including system configurations, business processes, user records, and proprietary workflows — as confidential. Information accessed or created during a consulting engagement is used exclusively to deliver that engagement. We do not disclose client information to third parties except as required to perform contracted services (e.g., subcontractors bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations) or as required by law.

Where a formal Statement of Work or consulting agreement is in place, the confidentiality terms of that agreement govern in addition to this policy.

Third-Party Platforms and Tools

Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). When your browser requests these fonts, Google may log your IP address per their own Privacy Policy. No other third-party scripts or services are loaded on this site.

During client engagements, we work within platforms you authorize us to access. These may include:

  • CRM & Event Tech: Salesforce, RainFocus
  • Support Platforms: Help Scout, Zendesk
  • Document & eSignature: DocuSign
  • Video & Media: Brightcove, Vimeo
  • Collaboration: Notion, GitHub, Slack, Google Workspace
  • AI Infrastructure: OpenAI and other LLM providers (used only with de-identified or client-approved data)

Data within these platforms is governed by their respective privacy policies. Our access is scoped to what is necessary to deliver your project.

Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions and email correspondence for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry or fulfill our contractual obligations.

Client project materials (documentation, configurations, notes) are retained for a reasonable period after project completion to support follow-up questions or future engagements. Credentials and access tokens are revoked or deleted promptly upon project completion unless retained access is explicitly agreed upon in a support arrangement.

If you would like your information removed, contact us at the address below and we will delete it within 30 days, subject to any legal or contractual retention requirements.

Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information, including:

  • Using encrypted channels (TLS) for all data transmission
  • Storing credentials and access tokens in password-managed, access-controlled vaults
  • Limiting access to client systems to only what is required for the specific engagement
  • Revoking access promptly upon project completion

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. If you have concerns about a specific piece of sensitive information, please contact us before sending it.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are directed to businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 years of age.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, email us at support@brytetech.solutions. We will respond within 30 days.

California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). As a small B2B consultancy, we do not sell personal data and do not meet the thresholds that trigger full CCPA compliance obligations — but we honor access and deletion requests regardless.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of our website or services after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
