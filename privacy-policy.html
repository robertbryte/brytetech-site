Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Introduction

Bryte Tech Solutions LLC ("Bryte Tech," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit brytetech.solutions or contact us, how we use it, and what choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Contact form submissions — your name, email address, company name, and message content when you fill out the contact form on our website.
  • Email correspondence — messages you send to our support or inquiry email addresses.

We do not use tracking pixels, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics platforms. Our website does not set any cookies.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and evaluate potential projects.
  • Deliver services you have engaged us to perform.
  • Send administrative communications related to an active engagement (invoices, project updates, scheduling).

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). When your browser requests these fonts, Google may log your IP address per their own Privacy Policy. No other third-party scripts or services are loaded on this site.

For active client engagements, we may use tools such as Notion, GitHub, or project management software to collaborate. Data shared within those tools is governed by their respective privacy policies and our client agreements.

Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions and email correspondence for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry or fulfill our contractual obligations. If you would like your information removed, contact us at the address below and we will delete it within 30 days.

Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. If you have concerns about a specific piece of sensitive information, please contact us before sending it.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are directed to businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 years of age.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, email us at support@brytetech.solutions. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of our website after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out:

  • Email: support@brytetech.solutions
  • Mailing address: Bryte Tech Solutions LLC, Simi Valley, CA
